ExplorersMap - credits for the data it ships

Map pieces (which parts of each zone's map exploring reveals):
  from OpenMap's reveal data, built from the game's own tables.
  https://github.com/openmap-world/openmap  (MIT, see LICENSES/OpenMap-LICENSE.txt)

Area names in English and Spanish:
  from LibBabble-SubZone-3.0 by Arith Hsu.
  https://github.com/arithmandar/LibBabble-SubZone-3.0  (MIT, see LICENSES/LibBabble-SubZone-3.0-LICENSE.txt)

Texture names used to match map pieces to areas:
  the community listfile, https://github.com/wowdev/wow-listfile

Zone level ranges and faction ownership (the Guide):
  as listed by the addon Zone Info: Forever and the Warcraft Wiki's "Zones by level" page.
